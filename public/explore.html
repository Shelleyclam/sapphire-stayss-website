Explore Yelagiri

Things to do nearby

Most of Yelagiri's parks, lakes and viewpoints are within 4 km of Sapphire Stayss. Distances are by road from our gate.

Right behind us

The Swamymalai trek

The trail to Swamymalai, the highest point in Yelagiri, starts about 500 m behind the resort. Go early in the morning when the air is cool, carry water and wear good shoes. Ask us for directions to the start.
