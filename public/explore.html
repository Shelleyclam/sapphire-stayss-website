Explore Yelagiri

Things to do nearby

Most of Yelagiri's lakes, parks, temples and viewpoints are within 4 km of Sapphire Stayss. Distances are by road from our gate. Timings and tickets change, so check before you go, or ask us.

Right behind us

The Swamymalai trek

Swamymalai is the highest peak in Yelagiri, at about 1,320 m. The trail starts in Mangalam, about 500 m behind the resort, and is roughly 6 km there and back. Most people take around 3 hours at an easy pace.

The trek is managed by the Forest Department and needs a ticket, so ask us to help you book. Start early, carry water and wear good shoes. Avoid it in heavy rain.
