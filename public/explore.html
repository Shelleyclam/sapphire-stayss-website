Things to do in Yelagiri

Most of Yelagiri's lakes, parks, temples and viewpoints are within 4 km of Sapphire Stayss in Mangalam. Pick what you are in the mood for, or the time of day, and we will show you what fits. Distances are by road from our gate.

Right behind us

The Swamymalai trek

Yelagiri's highest peak, at about 1,320 m. The trail climbs through forest to open rock at the top, with views over the hills and the plains of Tirupattur below. It is roughly 6 km there and back at an easy pace.

The trek is managed by the Forest Department and needs a ticket. Carry water, wear good shoes and avoid it in heavy rain.

AR Thanga Kottai

A palace-style hotel on 10 acres that is also open to day visitors. Wander through themed photo spots such as the Barque of Rome, the Balcony of Buckingham and the Parthenon, plus a vintage car, waterfalls, Pallava-style caves, a banyan tree over 100 years old, a 150-year-old Thanjavur painting, pedal boats and a kids' play area.

Best time
Visiting hours 9 AM to 6 PM; mornings are quieter
Time needed
1.5 to 2 hours
Good for
Families, photographers
Where
Kottur village

Tip Visitors buy an entry ticket at the gate (about ₹350 for adults and ₹200 for children when last checked). The swimming pool is only for hotel guests.

Jalagamparai Waterfalls

A forest waterfall below Yelagiri, at its best after the monsoon. The drive down and the short walk to the falls make it a good half-day outing.

Best time
Morning; fullest October to February
Time needed
Half a day
Good for
Nature lovers
Where
Below the Yelagiri hills, about 40 km by road

Tip Carry water and wear shoes with a good grip; rocks near the falls are slippery.
