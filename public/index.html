Ideas for your stay

Noon to noon

Check-in and check-out are both at 12 noon, so even one night gives you a full afternoon, evening and morning in the hills. Here's what your day could look like. These are only suggestions; spend it however you like. Anything marked "On request" is something we can arrange for you.

12:00Arrive

Settle in

Your room is ready at noon: air-conditioning, fresh linen, a kettle with tea and coffee, and a terrace over the garden.

14:30Afternoon

Boating or adventure parks

Take a boat out on Punganoor Lake at the Boat House, or spend the afternoon at Thrill Valley, Lake Zone or Wild Waves. All are about 3.5 km away. With children? Fundera bird park is 2 km and the Birds Park is 1.5 km.

17:00Late afternoon

Views from Nilavoor

Drive 2.5 km to Nilavoor View Point for wide views over the hills and valley as the day cools down.

19:00Evening

Fountain show at the lake

The musical fountain at the Nature Park, opposite the Boat House, starts at 7 PM every day. It is 3.5 km from us.

20:30Night

Campfire in the garden

End the day around a campfire in the garden. Let us know a day ahead and we will set it up; there is an extra charge. Celebrating? We can also arrange a cake and decorate your room.

On request · extra charge
06:30Early morning

Climb Swamymalai

The trek to Swamymalai, the highest point in Yelagiri, starts just 500 m behind the resort. Set off early for cool air and clear views, and be back in time for breakfast.

Starts behind the resort
08:30Morning

Breakfast is on us

Soft idly, coconut chutney and sambar in the glass-walled dining room, with the garden outside. Served 8:30 to 10 AM, complimentary for every guest.

10:30Before you leave

Chocolates and one last stop

Pick up homemade chocolates at the Chocolate Factory and Selfie Panda, 1 km away, or visit the Murugan Temple at 1.5 km. Check-out is at 12 noon.
