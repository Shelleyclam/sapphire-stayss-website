Our place

Sapphire Stayss sits in Mangalam, on the quieter side of Yelagiri, a few kilometres from the busy lake area. Mornings start with birdsong, and the Swamymalai trail begins almost at our gate.

We keep it small on purpose: a garden with swings and shady benches, clean and comfortable rooms, a good South Indian breakfast, and people who are there when you need something.

What we care about

Clean, comfortable rooms

Fresh linen, spotless bathrooms and hot water, every stay. It is the thing our guests mention most.

Help when you need it

Dinner ordered in, a campfire in the garden, a cake for a birthday, directions to the trek. Just ask us.

A quiet place to rest

No crowds and no loud music. Just the hills, the garden and time to slow down.
