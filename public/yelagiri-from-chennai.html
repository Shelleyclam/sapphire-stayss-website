How to reach Yelagiri from Chennai

Sapphire Stayss is 255 km by road from Chennai, a drive of 4½ to 5½ hours. Almost all of it is on the Chennai–Bengaluru highway; only the last stretch climbs the hills.

Ghat road

Distance is by road to our gate. Drive times are typical and depend on traffic.

The route

Chennai → Poonamallee → Sriperumbudur → Walajapet → Vellore → Ambur → Vaniyambadi → Ponneri → Yelagiri ghat road → Mangalam

Chennai to Vellore

Leave Chennai on NH 48 (the Bengaluru highway) through Poonamallee, Sriperumbudur and Walajapet to Vellore. Vellore, with its fort, is a good place for a break.

Vellore to Vaniyambadi

Stay on NH 48 through Ambur, known for its biryani, to Vaniyambadi.

Up to Yelagiri

Leave the highway at Vaniyambadi and follow the signs for Yelagiri through Ponneri, where the ghat road starts. Google Maps will show you the exact turn.

The last turn

In Mangalam, just before Mangalam Government School, turn left at the Sapphire Stayss name board.

The ghat road

The road up from Ponneri, at the foot of the hills climbs through 14 hairpin bends. It is a two-way road, so expect buses and cars coming down as you go up.

  • Climb in a low gear and use the engine to brake on the way down.
  • Sound your horn before blind bends, and don't overtake on them.
  • If you haven't driven it before, try to arrive in daylight.
  • Fill up on fuel and cash in the plains before you start the climb.

When you reach Yelagiri

At the top, the air is noticeably cooler than in the plains. Sapphire Stayss is in Mangalam, on the quieter side of Yelagiri at the foot of Swamymalai; open our Google Maps pin for the last few kilometres. Check-in is at 12 noon, so leaving Chennai early in the morning gets you here in time to check in and enjoy a full afternoon.

Weekends and holidays are busier on the highway and at Yelagiri's lake and parks, so allow extra time.

Without a car

By train: the nearest railway station is Jolarpettai Junction, about 20 km from Yelagiri, with trains from both Chennai and Bengaluru. Taxis and autos from the station take the same ghat road up from Ponneri.

By bus: take a long-distance bus to Vaniyambadi, Jolarpettai or Tirupattur on the plains. Local government buses run up the ghat road to Yelagiri; timings change, so check at the bus stand.

By air: the nearest big airports are Bengaluru and Chennai; continue by road or train as above.
