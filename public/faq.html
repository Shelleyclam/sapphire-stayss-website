Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in and check-out are both at 12 noon. If you need an early check-in or late check-out, message us and we will try to arrange it.

How far is Sapphire Stayss from Bangalore?

Sapphire Stayss is 167 km by road from Bengaluru, usually 3½ to 4 hours by car, via Hosur, Krishnagiri and Vaniyambadi, then up the ghat road from Ponneri. See how to reach Yelagiri from Bangalore.

How far is Sapphire Stayss from Chennai?

Sapphire Stayss is 255 km by road from Chennai, usually 4½ to 5½ hours by car, on the Chennai–Bengaluru highway via Vellore, Ambur and Vaniyambadi, then up the ghat road from Ponneri. See how to reach Yelagiri from Chennai.

What is the nearest railway station?

Jolarpettai Junction, about 20 km from Yelagiri by road. From the station you can take a taxi or auto up the ghat road. We don't run a station pickup, but message us and we will tell you the easiest way up from the station.

How do I find the resort in Mangalam?

In Mangalam, just before Mangalam Government School, turn left at the Sapphire Stayss name board. Or open our Google Maps pin.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A South Indian breakfast is complimentary for every guest, including anyone on an extra mattress. It is served 8:30 to 10 AM in our glass-walled dining room.

Do you serve lunch or dinner?

We do not run a restaurant, but we can order lunch or dinner for you from a good local restaurant and serve it at the property. Let us know in advance.

Is there a swimming pool?

No, Sapphire Stayss does not have a swimming pool.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed at Sapphire Stayss.

Can I bring children or add an extra bed?

Yes. Children under 6 years count as children; guests aged 6 and over count as adults. Each double bed sleeps two adults and one child, and the single bed sleeps one. We can add an extra mattress, and breakfast is included for the extra guest; ask us for the price. Every room page shows the maximum number of guests.

Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

Yes, both are free. Parking is inside the property.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 3 days before your check-in date and we refund your advance in full. If you cancel later than that, or do not arrive, the advance is not refunded. Bookings on Booking.com, Agoda or other sites follow that site's terms. Read the cancellation & refund policy.

How do I book?

WhatsApp or call us on +91 93639 41162 or +91 98844 77120, email sapphirestayin@gmail.com, or send your dates through our booking form. A 50% advance confirms your room and you pay the balance at check-in.

What ID do I need?

All adult guests need a valid government photo ID, such as an Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence or voter ID, at check-in.

Can you arrange a campfire, cake or decoration?

Yes. Campfires, birthday or anniversary cakes and room decoration can be arranged if you ask at least a day ahead. There is an extra charge; message us for prices.

When is the best time to visit Yelagiri?

Most visitors find November to February the most pleasant, with cool weather. March to May is the warmest time of year, and the Tamil Nadu tourism department's summer festival is usually held in May. July to September brings the monsoon rains. Jalagamparai Waterfalls, a half-day trip, is fullest from October to February.
