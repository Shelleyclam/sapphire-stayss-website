Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Sapphire Stayss collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you book or enquire: your name, phone number, email address, dates and number of guests.
  • ID details at check-in, as required by law.
  • Payment confirmations from our payment partner. Card and bank details are handled by the payment partner, not by us.

How we use it

We use your information only to manage your booking, contact you about your stay, meet legal requirements and improve our service. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services needed to run your booking: our booking system, our payment partner, and authorities when the law requires it.

This website

This website does not use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the map on the contact page is shown by Google Maps. When you tap a WhatsApp button, your message is sent through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, unless we must keep it by law. Contact us at sapphirestayin@gmail.com or +91 93639 41162.
