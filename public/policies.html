Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in and check-out are both at 12 noon. If you need an early check-in or late check-out, message us and we will try to arrange it.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A South Indian breakfast is complimentary for every guest, including anyone on an extra mattress. It is served 8:30 to 10 AM.

How do I pay?

For bookings made on this website, a 50% advance confirms your room. You pay the balance at check-in. Bookings made on Booking.com, Agoda or other sites follow the payment terms shown on that site.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 3 days before your check-in date and we refund your advance in full. If you cancel later than that, or do not arrive, the advance is not refunded.

Can I add an extra bed?

Yes. An extra mattress costs ₹200 a night and includes breakfast for the extra guest.

How many people can stay in a room?

Each double bed sleeps two adults and one child, and the single bed sleeps one. Every room page shows the maximum number of guests.

Who counts as a child?

Children under 6 years count as children. Guests aged 6 and over count as adults.

Do you serve lunch or dinner?

We do not run a restaurant, but we can order lunch or dinner for you from a good local restaurant and serve it at the property. Let us know in advance.

Can you arrange a campfire, cake or decoration?

Yes. Campfires, birthday or anniversary cakes and room decoration can be arranged if you ask at least a day ahead. Message us for prices.

Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

Yes, both are free. Parking is inside the property.

Are pets allowed?

Sorry, pets are not allowed.

Can I smoke?

Smoking is not allowed.

What ID do I need?

All adult guests need a valid government photo ID, such as an Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence or voter ID, at check-in.
