Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy applies to bookings made directly with Sapphire Stayss. Bookings made on Booking.com, Agoda or other travel sites follow the cancellation terms shown on that site.

Free cancellation

Cancel at least 3 days before your check-in date and we refund your advance in full.

Late cancellation and no-show

If you cancel less than 3 days before your check-in date, or do not arrive, the advance is not refunded.

Changing your dates

Message us as early as possible. We will move your booking to new dates if a room is available.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days of the cancellation being confirmed. The exact time depends on your bank.

If we have to cancel

If we cannot host you for reasons on our side, we refund everything you have paid.

How to cancel

Send us your booking details on WhatsApp at +91 93639 41162 or email sapphirestayin@gmail.com. We will confirm the cancellation in writing.
